Privacy Policy for Couch to Whale

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Couch to Whale ("the game", "we", "us") is a mobile game about predicting simulated crypto prices with pretend money. This policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The short version: you don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. The game sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase (in the EEA, UK and Switzerland only if you agree, and you can turn it off in Settings anywhere). Optional "watch an ad" rewards are provided by Google AdMob, which collects device information to show ads. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (pretend cash, bets, level, skills, trophies and settings) is saved on your device. It is not sent to us.

Android backup: if backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy the game's save to your Google account so it can be restored when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. Google stores that backup under its own privacy policy. We cannot see or access it.

2. Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how people play, and to find where the game is confusing or broken, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google.

What is collected:

  • Game events: for example starting a new game, opening a screen, starting or skipping the tutorial, switching from Simple mode to the full game, placing, selling or settling a bet with pretend money (which coin, contract length, side and pretend amount; several bets that settle together are sent as one summary), skipping ahead (and how many times), coming back to the game (and roughly how many hours you were away), buying pretend coins or decorations, putting pretend cash in the vault, getting a free coin drop, leveling up, prestiging, unlocking or resetting skills, picking a quest, earning a trophy or league prize, claiming a daily reward, opening the store, being offered, watching or declining a rewarded ad, answering the notification prompt, using an ad-free skip, and the result of an in-app purchase attempt. When you switch usage statistics off, one last event records that it was switched off.
  • Game properties: your level, which skill tree you chose, whether you use Simple mode, and whether you own Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or the Mogul Pack.
  • Device and app information that Firebase collects automatically: an app-instance identifier generated for this install, device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country or region (worked out from your IP address; your precise location is never collected), and when and how long the game is used. Firebase also records when the app is first opened and when sessions start.

What is not collected: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details. Analytics does not use the Android Advertising ID, and its ad personalization is turned off. (The rewarded ads in section 5 are separate and do use it.)

How it is used: only to understand and improve the game (for example, how many players finish the tutorial, or which features are used). It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not combined with other information to identify you.

How long it is kept: Google Analytics keeps this event data for a limited period (2 months by default) and then deletes it. Aggregated reports, which cannot identify you, may be kept longer.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where the law requires consent, usage statistics are only sent if you agree in Google's consent form (the same form that asks about ads), to storing and accessing information on your device for Google. Until the game knows your answer, nothing is sent. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.

Turning it off: open Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage stats" in the game and switch it off. The game then stops sending usage statistics from your device.

Google's processing of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. In-app purchases

The game offers optional one-time purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, 12-Hour Offline and Mogul Pack) and a repeatable purchase (10 Ad-free Skips). These are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your name, card number or billing address. The game only learns which items you own (and, for 10 Ad-free Skips, that a purchase went through), so it can unlock them or add the skips. A record of which skip purchases have already been added, and how many skips you have left, is kept on your device and in its Android backup so the same purchase is never counted twice. Real-money purchases never add pretend cash, coins or experience points directly. Remove Ads and the Mogul Pack only let you collect the rewards described in section 5 without watching the ad.

Google's handling of your payment information is covered by Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Payments privacy notice.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example, when a bet settles or a daily reward is ready) on your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn notifications off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch a video ad to skip ahead, reset your skills, win back part of an offline loss, get pretend cash when you're nearly broke, or earn a short 2× XP boost. Ads only play when you tap to watch one. These ads are provided by Google AdMob.

What AdMob collects: to show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob collects the Android Advertising ID, device information (such as model, operating system, language and screen size), approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads. This data is shared with Google, which uses it under its own policies, including to show ads that may be personalized to you where the law allows.

Your choices:

  • Consent (EU, UK and other regions where it's required): before any ad is shown, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalized ads. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • Remove Ads: the optional Remove Ads purchase, or the Mogul Pack (which includes it), gives every reward without showing an ad.

Ad content: ads are limited to a Teen (T) content rating, and gambling, betting and cryptocurrency ads are blocked.

Google explains how it uses data from apps that show its ads at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Children

Couch to Whale is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The game contains simulated gambling. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you would like their analytics data deleted, contact us (see below).

7. Your choices and rights

  • Stop analytics: switch off "Share anonymous usage stats" in Settings.
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game, or clear its data in your phone's Settings → Apps → Couch to Whale → Storage. To remove a backup copy, manage backups in your Google account.
  • Ask us about your data: depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access or delete personal information. Because we don't collect your name or contact details, we usually can't link analytics data to you. Contact us and we will help where we can.

8. Security

Your game save stays on your device and is protected by Android. Data sent to Google Analytics for Firebase travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is stored by Google.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting different information, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game or its store listing.
