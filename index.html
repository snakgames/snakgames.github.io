About the game

You have $100 of pretend money and a hunch. Will Biddy Coin finish above the line or below it? Make the call and watch it play out.

Start simple with Up or Down. Level up to unlock the full trading floor: Yes and No bets, selling early, more coins and longer bets.

  • Pick one of four skill trees and play your way
  • Ride hot streaks for bigger wins
  • Upgrade from a beat-up couch to a private island
  • Race rivals in weekly leagues and collect 69 trophies
  • Reach the top, then prestige for a fresh start with bonuses
  • The market keeps moving while you're away

Everything here is pretend: the money has no real value, you can't win real money or prizes, and the markets are simulated. It's a game, not financial advice. Optional purchases remove ads or add cosmetics and skips.
